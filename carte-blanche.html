About this package

Designed entirely around you. Nothing less.

The Carte Blanche exists for those who don't want to choose from a menu. Every element — the venue, the concept, the entertainment, the catering, the finishing touches — is designed from scratch in collaboration with you.

We've produced rooftop dinners with live orchestras, villa weekenders with celebrity DJs, themed warehouse parties for 500, and intimate suppers that felt like theatre. The common thread: nothing is off the table.

This package starts at €1,899 and scales with your vision. We'll give you a fully itemised proposal after our first conversation.

What's always included
  • Bespoke concept and creative direction
    We develop a unique visual and experiential concept built around your brief, aesthetic and personality.
  • Full venue sourcing and management
    Access to our private network of extraordinary venues across Mallorca and beyond.
  • Premium catering and open bar
    From canapé receptions to seated dinners and midnight feasts — menus created by our partner chefs.
  • Live entertainment (your choice)
    Celebrity DJ, live band, string quartet, fire performers, comedians — we have the connections.
  • Grand finale moment
    Fireworks, cold sparklers, confetti cannons — something to mark the end of an extraordinary night.
  • Personal event director
    Your own dedicated director manages everything from first call to final clear-up.
