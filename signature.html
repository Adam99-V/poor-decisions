About this package

Everything you need for a night that earns its own chapter

The Signature is where Poor Decisions really hits its stride. This is our most popular package for a reason — it covers everything from full venue transformation to a live DJ, catering for up to 75 guests, and a dedicated event team managing every moment.

Whether it's a landmark birthday, a corporate celebration, a hen do that gets talked about, or simply an excuse to throw the best party of the year — The Signature delivers.

You show up. We handle everything else.

What's included
  • Full venue transformation
    We redesign the space — linens, lighting, floral, signage and decor — all matched to your theme and colour palette.
  • Professional DJ and lighting rig
    A curated DJ performing for up to 6 hours with a full lighting setup including wash, spots and effects.
  • Catering for up to 75 guests
    Welcome drinks, canapés and a dinner or grazing format. Menu designed in consultation with you.
  • Photo experience station
    Staffed photo booth with props, instant prints and a digital gallery shared within 24 hours.
  • Dedicated event team
    A lead coordinator and supporting staff on-site for the entire event duration.
  • Full setup and breakdown
    We arrive hours before your guests, and we're gone before morning. You handle nothing.
