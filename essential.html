About this package

A refined foundation for an unforgettable evening

The Essential is our entry-level package — but there's nothing basic about it. Designed for intimate gatherings of up to 30 guests, it sets the tone beautifully without overwhelming the senses.

Perfect for milestone birthdays, anniversary dinners, farewell parties, or any occasion where the company is the centrepiece and you just need the atmosphere to match.

Our team handles every detail — from arrival setup to final tidy — so you can be a guest at your own event.

What's included
  • Full venue styling
    Linens, centrepieces, candles and ambient decor tailored to your brief.
  • Welcome drinks on arrival
    A curated welcome drink for all guests — prosecco, cocktail or mocktail.
  • Curated music playlist
    A professionally curated Spotify playlist matched to your vibe and moment.
  • Dedicated event coordinator
    One of our team on-site throughout the entire event.
  • Setup and breakdown
    We arrive early, we leave late, you don't lift a finger.
Good to know

This package works with any venue in Mallorca. Don't have a venue in mind? We have relationships with some of the finest private spaces on the island and can recommend options to suit your group and budget.

Want to scale up? Everything in The Essential is included in our Signature and Carte Blanche packages.
