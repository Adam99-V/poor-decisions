The experience

Albufeira's Strip.
Start to Finish.

Albufeira is the party capital of Portugal — and the Strip (Avenida Sá Carneiro) is where it all happens. 800 metres of neon-lit bars, clubs and chaos running until sunrise every night of the week in peak season. We know every inch of it.

Your night starts at one of the Strip's best bars, where you check in with your host, meet the group and kick off with free shots and drinking games. From there, your rep leads the group through 4 to 6 of the best stops on the strip — bars, clubs and the kind of places you wouldn't find on your own — ending at one of Albufeira's top nightclubs.

Free shots at every stop, queue jump entry throughout, and a rep who's done this enough times to know exactly when to move and where to go next. No planning, no getting lost, no paying over the odds at the door.

Where you'll end up

Albufeira's Best
Clubs on the Rotation

The final club changes by night, but it's always one of the Strip's top venues. Here's what's on the rotation:

Kiss Disco Club
Rua Vasco da Gama — Portugal's 2nd oldest club
An Albufeira institution. Massive dancefloor, international DJs, live acts and the kind of late-night energy that keeps going until sunrise. A rite of passage.
Club Heaven
The Strip — Opens 2am
One of the Strip's biggest clubs. International DJs, live shows, cheap drinks and a crowd that's there for it. Famous for their Sunset Party and boat party events in summer.
Libertos
The Strip — Est. 1985
Albufeira's most glamorous club — outdoor terrace, VIP sky lounge, premium cocktails and a stylish crowd. More upscale than the rest of the strip and worth every minute.
Club Vida
The Strip — Multi-room
Multiple rooms playing house, techno, R&B and hip-hop. Excellent VIP areas and a top choice for groups. One of the Strip's most popular venues night after night.
Kadoc
Outside Albufeira — 7,000 capacity, 5 dancefloors
Portugal's most famous superclub — a short ride from the Strip with capacity for 7,000 across 5 dancefloors and a sub-tropical garden terrace. Regularly hosts big-name international DJs. Available as an optional add-on or upgrade for groups.
