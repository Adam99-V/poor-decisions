Nightclub — Ayia Napa Strip
Castle Club
Ayia Napa's biggest superclub — ranked in DJ Mag's top 100 in the world. Five arenas, each with its own vibe: pop, R&B/hip-hop, house/techno, a chillout terrace, and a luxury VIP section. Capacity 4,000+. The anchor of any Napa nightlife week.

Pool party — Pambos Napa Rocks Hotel
Pambos Pool Party
The biggest and longest-running pool party in Ayia Napa. Top DJs playing house, R&B and classics every Thursday afternoon — sip cocktails in the sunshine, cool off in the pool, and understand why the strip goes quiet on Thursday evenings. VIP tables available.

03
Nightclub — Main Square
Carwash Disco
The largest club in Cyprus and a Napa institution since 1996. Carwash plays cheesy hits from the 70s, 80s, 90s and beyond — the crowd is everyone, the vibe is anything goes, and it runs until 5–6am. Iconic. Non-negotiable.
Opens
Daily from 1:30am
04
Pool party — Pambos Napa Rocks Hotel
Sunset Sessions
Ayia Napa's newest and fastest-growing pool party. Think Ibiza pool party energy on a Sunday afternoon — sick beats, great vibes, and a crowd that's there for it. Swimwear, sunglasses and good energy only. VIP cabanas available on the day.
Runs every
Sunday afternoon
05
Nightclub — Ayia Napa Strip
Club Ice
One of Napa's busiest clubs — two arenas with state-of-the-art sound and lighting plus a signature ice wall. Famous for its themed nights: foam parties, paint parties, popcorn nights. Always packed, always a laugh. Monday foam party is a rite of passage.
Open
Nightly, peak season
06
Boat party — Ayia Napa Harbour
Fantasy Boat Party
Ayia Napa's most popular boat party — running multiple times a week from the harbour. DJ, drinks, swimming stops in the Mediterranean and a crowd that's there to party. One of the best daytime experiences on the island, full stop.
Runs
Multiple days per week
07
Nightclub — Ayia Napa Square
Soho Club
Sleek underground club just off the main square. Multiple floors, each playing a different genre — dance, R&B and pop. VIP booths, bottle service and a crowd that shows up dressed. One of Napa's more upscale club experiences.
Open
Nightly, peak season
08
Rooftop party — Aqua Club / Various
Vice Parties on the Roof
An open-air rooftop party experience that's become one of Ayia Napa's most talked-about nights. Friday and Saturday events with guest DJs, a view over the strip, and the kind of atmosphere that's hard to find anywhere else on the island.
Runs
Fri & Sat nights
09
Nightclub — Ayia Napa Square
Club Sin
Small, energetic and always busy in peak season. Club Sin ditches the generic chart music in favour of urban hits — hip-hop, bashment, dancehall, grime and Afrobeats. Small VIP area at the back. Open June to August only — peak season exclusive.
Open
June — August only
10
Nightclub — Ayia Napa Strip
Black N White
One of Ayia Napa's most consistent clubs — open from June through to the end of October, and one of the few venues that keeps going into the shoulder season. A solid, reliable night out with a mix of dance, urban and party anthems.
Open
June — October
