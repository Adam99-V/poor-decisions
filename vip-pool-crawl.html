The experience

Vegas Pool Parties.
Done Properly.

Get ready for a full Las Vegas nightlife experience without the hassle of planning the night yourself. Your evening starts at a buzzing bar on the Strip, where you'll check in with your professional party host and meet the rest of the group.

Las Vegas isn't just a nighttime city. The dayclubs and pool parties on the Strip are world-class — and most people either don't know about them or can't get into the best ones without connections. We fix both problems.

Your VIP Pool Crawl hits 2 to 3 of Vegas's best pool parties across the day, with VIP cabana access, bottle service options and a host who gets you in and set up. No queuing in the sun, no turning up and being told the cabanas are sold out.

▲ Bottle service available

Upgrade to a full bottle package at any of the pool venues. Speak to your host when booking and we'll get it arranged in advance.
