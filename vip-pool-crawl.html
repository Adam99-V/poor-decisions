The experience

Vegas by day.Done properly.

Vegas daytime is its own scene — world-class DJs, pool decks the size of football pitches and a crowd that starts at 11am and doesn't stop. Your VIP Pool Crawl gets you into the best of it without queuing in the desert heat.

Meet your host, get your wristbands, and get straight into the first dayclub with express entry. From there the group moves between the Strip's top pool parties — Encore Beach Club, Marquee Dayclub, TAO Beach, Wet Republic — with entry sorted at every door.

Want to go big? VIP cabanas and daybeds with bottle service can be arranged for your group in advance. Just tell us when you book.
