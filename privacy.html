Privacy policy.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Poor Decisions collects, why we collect it and what we do with it. It applies to poordecisions.co, our enquiry and booking forms, and lead forms you complete on Facebook or Instagram.

Who we are

Poor Decisions is operated by Poor Decisions Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom. We arrange nightlife experiences in Las Vegas, including club and pool party guest list entry and open bar packages. You can reach us at official.poordecisions@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +44 7849 871895.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Trip details: your travel dates, group size, the venues and music you like, and anything else you tell us about your plans.
  • Booking details: what you booked, when, and how much you paid. Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Website usage: pages visited, the device and browser used, and how you reached us. This is collected through cookies and similar tools from Google Analytics and Meta.

How we collect it

You give us information when you fill in a form on our website, complete a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, build a trip in the Vegas planner, make a booking, or message us. Usage information is collected automatically when you browse the site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a suggested lineup for your trip.
  • To arrange your booking, including adding you to venue guest lists.
  • To take payment and send confirmations.
  • To contact you about your trip or a booking you started.
  • To understand how our website and adverts perform so we can improve them.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number through a form, you agree that we can text or call you on that number about your enquiry, your trip and our Las Vegas packages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of buying anything. Reply STOP to any text at any time and we will stop messaging you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to deliver what you asked for:

  • Venues and nightlife partners in Las Vegas, so your name can be added to guest lists and your booking honoured.
  • Stripe, to process payments.
  • Google and Meta, which provide our analytics, advertising, forms and spreadsheets.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. We keep booking and payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require, usually six years.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Email official.poordecisions@gmail.com and we will deal with it within 30 days. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under local law, such as the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws. We will honour those rights whichever country you contact us from.

Age

Our Las Vegas experiences are for adults aged 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
