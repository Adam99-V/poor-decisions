The experience

A Full Vegas Night Out.
Without the Stress.

Get ready for a full Las Vegas nightlife experience without the hassle of planning the night yourself. Your evening starts at a buzzing bar on the Strip, where you'll check in with your professional party host and meet the rest of the group.

This is where the night kicks off properly — your party host gets the group going with high-energy drinking games and a 1-hour open bar with bottomless vodka drinks included every night. After the first venue, the group boards the party bus. Music, lights, drinks and games as you cruise through the city between stops. Throughout the night your host guides the group through 3 to 4 venues: bars, lounges, rooftop-style spots, and one of Vegas' leading nightclubs to finish.
